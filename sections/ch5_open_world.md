## Main path summary

Free-roam on Facility 3826 grounds. The open world spans two access windows (Ch.5 before Theatre, and Open World 2 after Pavlov). This is where most weapon-mod blueprints and Lootyagin chest rewards are accumulated. The story continues when you enter the Sechenov Center / Theatre zone — but **don't go there until you've done all available Polygons**.

## ⚠️ Critical missable window — Lootyagin chests + Chirpers

**ALL open-world content — Polygons, Lootyagin chests, chirpers, corpses, Polygon blueprints — locks permanently** when you trigger "Sky's the Limit" quest in Ch.9. Every open-world item must be collected before that trigger.

> **Cross-system dependency** — see `dependencies.md` DEP-004: Sky's the Limit locks all 12 Polygons, all 24 Lootyagin chests, and all open-world blueprint acquisitions permanently.

**Latest safe window for open-world content = before Ch.9 "Sky's the Limit" trigger (post-Pavlov open world 2).**

However, do Polygons in this window (Ch.5) as many as possible — fewer to clean up in the Ch.7–8 window.

## Polygon routing (recommended order)

Complete these Polygons in Ch.5 before triggering the Theatre (Ch.6):
- **Polygon 1** — accessible from VDNH outdoor; do it there or now
- **Polygon 2** — do now
- **Polygon 6** — requires "Bug in the System" quest active; do now
- **Polygon 8** — may require "Bug in the System" started [Single source — verify]; do now
- **Polygon 10** — camera tower hack; do now

Leave these for **Open World 2** (after Pavlov, Ch.7–8 window):
- **Polygon 9** — camera hack after clearing sprout infestation (sewer boilers); single entry in village center. No Pavlov key required. [CORRECTED live 2026-05-16 — prior note "cookie-key door from Pavlov" was unsourced and wrong]

- **Polygon 12** — unlocks only after "Petrov of Opera" quest complete (post-Theatre)

Polygons 3, 4, 5, 7: open; do them whenever convenient.

> **Cross-system dependency** — see `dependencies.md` DEP-002: Polygon 8 (TG8) + all 5 weapon mods inside it permanently lock after Theater PoNR (edge E39). Must be completed during "Bug in the System" quest before entering the Theatre.

## Hawk relay disable — standard procedure

Before farming or entering any Polygon, shoot down the local Hawk relay. See `mechanics.md` — HAWK Relay System for the full procedure. Inside Polygons, robots do not respawn — stay dead.

> **Cross-system dependency** — see `dependencies.md` DEP-005: HAWK relay disable suppresses Pchela respawns and Dandelion cameras — shared precondition for both safe farming and safe Polygon entry.

Community recommends **binding Quick-cartridge swap** to a side mouse button (thumb button) at this point. The default middle-click-on-wheel cartridge swap is too slow for open-world encounters. See `controls.md`.

The **Frost + Pashtet melee** combination becomes the dominant build in the open world. If you haven't unlocked Frostbite Absolute Zero yet, do it now from the nearest NORA terminal. See `items/builds.md` Build A.
