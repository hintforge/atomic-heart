### Endings — structural note (spoiler: none — public knowledge)

There are **two endings** accessible in a single playthrough:
- An early-exit outcome triggered by a specific dialogue choice at Granny Zina (very short — ~30 seconds of content).
- A full-length canon ending after a multi-stage encounter.

Both can be claimed in one playthrough by **saving before Granny Zina's dialogue choice**, completing one, then reloading for the other. Keep a manual save here.

### Achievement — Armageddon difficulty exploit

The "Atomic Heart" achievement requires clearing the **canon ending** on **Armageddon difficulty**. A documented exploit: play the entire game on **Local Failure (Normal)**, then **change difficulty to Armageddon** just before the final encounter's killing blow. [Confirmed: PSNProfiles trophy guide — still working as of Patch 1.29]

If going for 100% achievements, this can save a full Armageddon playthrough.

### Scanner achievement (missable — final window)

During the Ch.10 final encounter, **scan both combatants individually** using your scanner. This is the only valid window for the "Scanner" achievement — an earlier opportunity in the prologue does NOT count. See `dependencies.md` DEP-012.
