## Point of no return

The fall through the glass floor at the end of the prologue is **permanent** — the exit to Vavilov locks `chelomey_city_prologue` immediately. No collectibles carry over. Confirm you've hit the phone booth before triggering the fall.

**Scanner achievement note:** if you scan any figures during the prologue, that does NOT count toward the "Scanner" achievement. The only valid window is in Ch.10. See `dependencies.md` DEP-012.

Edge: `chelomey_city_prologue` → `vavilov_entrance` (permanent, one-way) — see `nav/architecture.md`.
