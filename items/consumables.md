## Usage notes

- **Juggler character upgrade** (see `upgrades.md`) lets you use Neuromed capsules without lowering your weapon — strongly recommended before Ch.6 Theatre fights.
- **Auto-heal setting** (Patch 1.14.4.0, Settings > Accessibility) auto-uses Small Neuromed when HP drops low. Recommended off for Neuropolymer economy control, but useful if you forget to heal manually.
- **Biomaterials** (for Neuromed crafting) are organic-enemy-heavy in Pavlov — stock up before leaving.

---

## "Chemist" achievement

Craft **one of each** of the 6 consumable types. All 6 must be crafted in a single playthrough. Blueprints for all 6 are available by Ch.8 (Pavlov) at the latest.
