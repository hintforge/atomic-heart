## Entry point

**Not connected to base-game open world.** Launched from **Main Menu → New Game → Annihilation Instinct** (select after owning DLC). Opens its own save slot. Plays the canonical "P-3 sides with Sechenov" base-game ending as an intro cutscene, then drops into the Lyogkaya Hotel.

_source: Compass P3 deep research 2026-05-09 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_
[Confirmed: MP1st, Steam 100% guide, notesread — multiple sources]

## Exit / outgoing edges

No connection back to base-game zones. The DLC is a self-contained branch; the final cutscene ends the DLC session. Finishing the DLC does not unlock anything in the base game.

## NORA stations

Present — DLC has NORA stations for saving and upgrading. **Note: NORA forgets crafting blueprints in the DLC.** If you dismantle the PM Makarov or KS-23 shotgun (received as quest rewards), you cannot rebuild them. Do not dismantle either starting weapon.

_source: Compass P3 deep research 2026-05-09 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct_
[RU source: StopGame, walkthroughs.games; confirmed across EN and RU walkthroughs]

## DLC loadout constraints

- **Charles absent** for ~45 minutes after the DLC opening.
- **Glove returns from Lebedev with only Techno-Stasis + Polymeric Shield** slots available. Shok, Frostbite, Mass Telekinesis, and Polymer Jet are unavailable for the entire DLC.
- Character, Energy, and Shok passive trees come **pre-fully-upgraded** — no need to spend Neuropolymer on them.
- All weapons cap at **Level 2** (base game caps at Level 4).

_source: Compass P3 deep research 2026-05-09 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct_

## Traversal

A **Strekoza dragonfly aircraft** is the DLC's substitute for the Moskvitch. It connects the three main areas: Lyogkaya Hotel / village swamp, Freedom Park (Lebedev's tower), and the Mendeleev Memorial / aircraft hangar. This is the de-facto fast travel; English guides underplay it.

## Sequential gates

1. **Hotel Lyogkaya — pod escape** — P-3 wakes in a hotel pod; NORA is "unhinged"; navigate pod bridges by entering pods and hitting interior panels to unlock the next bridge connection.
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · missable: no · point_of_no_return: none
   - source: walkthroughs.games puzzle guide (EN/RU, 2023)

2. **Hotel exit — Klusha pickup** ⚠️ — Pick up the Klusha from an impaled robot at hotel exit after dialog with NORA. First weapon of the DLC.
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · missable: no · point_of_no_return: none
   - source: notesread/StopGame (EN, 2023)

3. **Village swamp traversal** — Cross flooded village to reach Freedom Park; talking goose appears but is harmless.
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · missable: no
   - source: notesread (EN, 2023)

4. **Lebedev's tower (*It's Complicated* / *Divide et Impera!*)** — Reach Lebedev's tower in Freedom Park; pick up the Secateur from a table on the lower floor (Terentyev). Sit in Lebedev's chair to unlock Techno-Stasis + Polymeric Shield.
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · missable: no · point_of_no_return: none
   - source: notesread (EN, 2023); walkthroughs.games (EN/RU, 2023)
   - ⚠️ *Before leaving Freedom Park*: explore for **Klusha Handle blueprint** — last safe point before complex descent.

5. **Surface BEA-D collection (*Surface Approach* — 3 BEA-Ds)** — Two sub-quests: *Scarlet Flower* (greenhouse), *Life Is Like a Box of Chocolates* (bus-stop park). First BEA-D earns PM Makarov, second earns KS-23 shotgun.
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · missable: no · point_of_no_return: none
   - source: wotpack (RU/EN, 2023)
   - ⚠️ Collect **Klusha Blade blueprint** (yellow rooftop chest near first BUS-A dome) before this mission completes.

6. **Underground BEA-D collection (*Magnetohod BUS-A* / *Complex Approach* — 5 underground BEA-Ds)** — Descend into the Mendeleev Complex interior. One BEA-D unlocks Eleanor at the magnet accelerator console. Side area: ORB Lab (*Weird Science* achievement).
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · missable: no
   - source: wotpack (RU/EN, 2023), notesread (EN, 2023)
   - ⚠️ Collect **Secateur Magazine + Energy Supercharger blueprints** (circular corridor near Mendeleev statue) before boss elevator — last safe point.

7. **BEA-D Colossus boss fight (phase 1)** — First boss encounter at the Mendeleev statue hall (white phase). [enemy-tier: 3 — see warning_tiers.md before reading combat details]
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 3 · point_of_no_return: none
   - source: walkthroughs.games boss guide (EN/RU, 2023)

8. **Return to Lebedev (*Desperate Measures*)** — Return to Lebedev's lab, phone Sechenov, then return to the Mendeleev Memorial.
   - spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 0 · point_of_no_return: none
   - source: wotpack (EN, 2023); notesread (EN, 2023)

9. **NORA reactor / recombination puzzle (*Eleanor's Heart*)** — Enter NORA's brain reactor. Solve the recombination-scheme orb puzzle. [puzzle-tier: 2 if hints needed — see puzzle notes below]
   - spoiler: dlc:annihilation_instinct · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · missable: no

10. **Final BEA-D Colossus fight (3 phases)** — Final boss fight. Phase 3 adds electrified spark sources. [enemy-tier: 3]

## Recombination puzzle notes (puzzle-tier: 2)

The recombination puzzle in *Eleanor's Heart*: 5 chains of 1/2/3/4/5 colored BEA-Ds set against a floor diagram. No penalty for misplacement — you can freely rearrange until the pattern is correct.

## Common confusions

- **Dismantle warning:** Do not dismantle the PM or KS-23 to fund upgrades — NORA does not relearn those blueprints in the DLC. You cannot rebuild them. (RU source: StopGame, walkthroughs.games)
- **Strekoza availability:** The dragonfly aircraft becomes available early; use it to travel between zones rather than running cross-map on foot.
- **Maximum Strength achievement:** requires both Klusha and Secateur blueprints found and both weapons upgraded to max before the final fight. If a blueprint was missed, the achievement is locked for this DLC run.
- **DLC save isolation:** DLC saves are completely separate from base-game saves. DLC completion does not modify the base game in any way.
