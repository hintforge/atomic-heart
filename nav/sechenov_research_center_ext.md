## Sequential Gates

1. **Outer Scaffolding Climb** (trigger) — climb scaffolding to the first roof level.
2. **Second-Roof Surveillance Hub + Cookie Key** (item-pickup) — Cookie Key disk is on a desk beside a corpse; opens Petrov's cutscene door inside the building.
3. **Drop into Building** (trigger) — enter sechenov_research_center_int.

## TG8 Access Window

TG8 requires activating three Volan cameras: one at the front of the road, one to the right of the building, and one on the rooftop. This is best done during the "Bug in the System" quest while the Research Center is active. **Window closes permanently at E39 (Theater PoNR).**
