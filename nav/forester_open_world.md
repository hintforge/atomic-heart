## Entry / Exit

- **Entry (E12):** Elevator from vavilov_birch_distribution_hall (permanent PoNR on entry — Vavilov sealed).
- **Exit (E15):** Train at Lesnaya Maglev Station. **PERMANENT POINT OF NO RETURN** — forester open world and all sub-zones sealed; Vavilov already sealed. [C:5]

**NORA booths:** Multiple red-roof mushroom safe-room huts along the main road — visible from a distance. [C:3] Also in granny_zina_hut (confirmed). The Volan tower itself has no NORA — use the nearest roadside hut.

## Before Leaving

> Once the train departs from Lesnaya Station, forester_open_world is permanently sealed. Collect all chirpers, talking deads, and blueprints in this zone and in granny_zina_hut before boarding. The train ticket must be the valid monthly pass from the Telemail rooftop (NOT the lower-right statue corpse).

## Story Progression Gates

1. **Volan Tower Attempt** (trigger) — approach the white spiral tower; overriding the white pedestal fails until Granny Zina's codes are provided.
2. **Granny Zina Encounter** (hub-spoke, E13) — interact with the hand-washing station at the empty farm; Granny's hut walks in. Granny provides the Volan override codes after the cutscene.
3. **Volan Tower Override** (E14, sdc2_volan_tower) — return with codes; hijack the gate camera, open the road north.
4. **Train Ticket Acquisition** (E15 prerequisite) — go to the Telemail post office (south-southwest of Lesnaya). Enter the second-floor open window, then swim up the polymer pipe to the roof. The rooftop corpse holds the valid monthly pass.
5. **Lesnaya Station (E15, PoNR)** — present the valid ticket to conductor Rafik; board the train.

## Train Ticket Detail (E15)

- **Valid ticket:** rooftop corpse at the **Telemail post office** (two-story, fire engine outside) — reach via second-floor open window + polymer swim up the pipe to the roof.
- **Expired ticket:** lower-right statue-base corpse at Lesnaya Station — conductor refuses this one.
- Post office is in forester_open_world, not in lesnaya_maglev_station zone. [C:5/2] [translated from: Russian]

## Common Confusions

- Following the objective marker straight to the station and missing the Telemail rooftop.
- Trying to bring the expired statue-base ticket — the conductor refuses it.
- The Volan tower cannot be overridden on the first visit — Granny's codes are required first.
- Cameras here trigger respawning robot waves until disabled at the HAWK relay.
- Shooting the train conductor (Rafik) permanently disables the train.
