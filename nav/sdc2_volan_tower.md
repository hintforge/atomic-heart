## Entry / Exit

- **Entry (E14):** Elevator at the base of the white spiral tower in forester_open_world. Bidirectional.
- **Exit (E14):** Elevator down — returns to forester_open_world.

**NORA booth:** None inside the tower. Use the nearest roadside mushroom-hut NORA. [C:2]

## Sequential Gates

1. **Ride Lift to Top** (trigger) — ascend to the glass-room at the tower's peak.
2. **First Override Attempt** (trigger) — interact with the white pedestal device; fails until Granny Zina's codes are provided after her cutscene.
3. **Post-Granny Return** (trigger) — return with codes; hijack the gate camera and open the road north, granting access to Lesnaya Station.

## Optional Branch

- Top-floor mattress: Chirper collectible #27 on the top floor.

## Common Confusions

- Players try to override the Volan pedestal before meeting Granny Zina and assume it's bugged — it requires her codes first.
