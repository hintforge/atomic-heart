# Nav — Vavilov Internal Maglev Station

**zone-id:** vavilov_maglev_station_solnechnaya_int
**status:** research-integrated
**research_run:** P2 (Compass/Deep Research 2026-05-08)

## Entry / Exit

- **Entry (E03):** vavilov_entry_road.
- **Exit (E04):** Cable-car booth lever → vavilov_maglev_tunnels. Story-gate; point of no return after lever pull — cannot return to the road.

**NORA booth:** YES — second safe room past the polymer swim tunnel (red-fridge NORA vendor with a large blueprint chest). **This is the first named NORA in the game** — unlocks the Electro pistol blueprint. [C:5]

## On Entry

NORA is through the polymer swim and past a safe room. If you haven't hit it yet, swim the polymer tunnel before pulling the cable-car lever.

## Sequential Gates

1. **Valve Door** (puzzle) — turn the wall valve to open the entry door; a chirper sits beside the valve. [C:3]
2. **First-Floor Office** (item-pickup) — room accessed through a broken window on the inner wall; chirper on a desk.
3. **Schematic Lock Booth (E04/E05 gate)** (puzzle) — disc-style lock on the cable-car operator booth; schematic note needed. Code → `../puzzles/combination_locks.md`. The schematic note (paper) is on the cable-car operator's corpse in a side niche off the main tunnel (next zone) — reach it via the Shok-triggered magnetized shutter door, then take the side niche. [C:5/2] [translated from: Russian]
4. **Tunnel Sidewalk / Supply Room** (item-pickup + door) — Shok the magnetized shutter door to reach the side niche with the operator corpse and the schematic. Supply Room Key on a Level-3 corridor corpse opens a separate supply room. [C:5]
5. **Polymer Swim Tunnel** (trigger) — dive through the polymer pool; a second safe room with a Fox-axe blueprint chest is beyond it. **NORA is in this safe room.**
6. **Cable-Car Lever** (cutscene, point of no return) — pull the lever inside the booth; cutscene drops P-3 into a pit. Cannot return to the road after this point.

## Optional Branches

- **Supply Room:** opened with the Supply Room Key from the Level-3 corridor corpse. Side loot; not story-required.

## Common Confusions

- The schematic note is in the side niche players miss — look for the magnetized shutter door near the tunnel sidewalk and use Shok to open it.
- The disc pattern code: the "C" shape is formed by three dots in the upper-left of the disc. See `../puzzles/combination_locks.md` for the full hint ladder.
- Entering the wrong elevator cab drops you at a dead end — the correct cab is the one the lever controls.
