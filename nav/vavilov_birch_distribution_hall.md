## Entry / Exit

- **Entry (E07):** From vavilov_seed_bank (bidirectional) or from any of the four workshops.
- **Exit (E12):** Elevator to forester_open_world after all four canisters placed. **PERMANENT POINT OF NO RETURN** — all Vavilov chirpers, blueprints, and talking-dead collectibles are locked forever once the elevator is taken. Charles warns of the PoNR. [C:6/2]

**NORA booth:** YES — Distribution-Hall NORA between canister return runs (the "real" PEC-4 birchtree altar with four canister sockets at its base). [C:3]

## Before Leaving

> This is the last chance to collect all Vavilov-zone items. The PoNR (elevator) is permanent. Sweep all four workshops and the distribution hall before placing the final canisters and taking the elevator.

## Sequential Gates

1. **Birchtree Atrium** (trigger) — central hub with PEC-4 birch sculpture (the one with the canister-socket ring). NORA vendor accessible throughout.
2. **Two-Candle Door** (puzzle) — insert two cryo orbs ("candles") into the cylinder tube to open the East Wing red door. Candles from the central pillar and a nearby corpse cell. [C:4]
3. **East Wing** (hub-spoke, bidirectional) — leads to vavilov_hot_workshop (E08) and vavilov_algae_workshop (via E09).
4. **West Wing** (hub-spoke, bidirectional) — opens after first two canisters returned. Leads to vavilov_pesticide_workshop (E10) and vavilov_cold_workshop (via E11).
5. **Petrov Cutscene Room** (cutscene) — after enough canisters, a cutscene plays in a side room. [C:3]
6. **Final Ascent** (story-gate, PERMANENT PoNR) — elevator to the surface. Do not take until all Vavilov collectibles are collected.

## Workshop Order

- Workshop pairs can be done in any order within each wing (East or West), but the West Wing door is gated behind two canisters.
- Recommended order: both East Wing workshops → return both canisters → West Wing opens → both West Wing workshops → return final canisters → PoNR warning → elevator.

## Common Confusions

- Trying to enter the West Wing before two canisters are returned — the wing door won't open.
- Missing the PoNR Charles warning and taking the elevator before clearing all zones.
