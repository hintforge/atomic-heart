- "The second 4-button code near the morgue elevator" — this does not exist. The elevator is just button "34". The other combination lock players reference is on the **morgue second-floor exit** (a different gate, in pavlov_morgue).
- Attacking the inactive Plyusch specimens in Surgical Lab #1 triggers them — don't.
- Disambiguation from vdnh_pavlov_floor: this is a real hospital with bloody handprints, hostile activity, and MRI machines; VDNH Pavlov floor has clean diorama beds and exhibit placards.
