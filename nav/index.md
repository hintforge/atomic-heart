## Routing Rules (apply to ALL nav files in this folder)

### NEVER use left/right — anchor to features

- The **zone entrance** (loading point, first NORA booth, first chamber) is the canonical reference.
- Describe forks by what they *contain* or *lead to*, not by direction.
  - OK: "From the entrance, the path toward the locked relay door is the main route."
  - OK: "Take the upper walkway / lower corridor / path through the broken blast door."
  - NOT OK: "Go right." / "Take the left fork."
- Cardinal directions (N/S/E/W) are acceptable when the in-game map shows them.

### Flag NORA booth locations on zone entry

Every zone's first NORA booth is flagged in the zone file's "On Entry" or "NORA booth" section. Save/upgrade stations are safety-critical — never withhold them.

### Hint format on zone entry

When P-3 arrives at a zone, **before any puzzle solutions**, give 2–3 short nav/anti-backtrack tips:
- Lead with collectible/chest order if the zone has one.
- Call out any "tempting shortcut that skips a collectible."
- Call out any "non-obvious correct exit."
- 3 numbered tips max.

## Scaffold-file fallback

If a zone's content seems incomplete for a specific question, web-search before asking a clarifying question. Nav-class questions default search-first.
